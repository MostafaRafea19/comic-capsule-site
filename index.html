Comic Capsule — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

Comic Capsule is a personal content-publishing tool operated by an individual creator. It exists for a single purpose: to publish short videos to social media accounts that the operator personally owns and controls.

Who this applies to

This application is not offered to the public. It has no sign-up, no user accounts, and no third-party users. It is used exclusively by its operator to post to the operator's own YouTube channel, Facebook Page, Instagram account, and TikTok account.

What information is collected

The application does not collect, request, store, or process personal information from any third party. Specifically, it does not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, phone numbers, or postal addresses of any other person
  • Contact lists, friend lists, or follower data
  • Messages, comments, or private communications
  • Location data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Any data belonging to visitors, viewers, or followers of the published content

What the application does access

Using access tokens granted by the operator for the operator's own accounts, the application:

  • Uploads video files and their captions to the operator's own social media accounts
  • Reads public statistics (such as view counts) for the operator's own published videos, and for publicly available videos on other public channels, in order to measure performance

All such access is limited to accounts the operator owns or to information that is already public.

Where information is stored

Operational data — video files, generated captions, and publishing records — is stored locally on the operator's own computer. Video files are temporarily hosted on private cloud storage solely to enable uploading, and are removed once publishing is complete. No database of personal information about other people is created or maintained.

Sharing with others

No information is sold, rented, traded, or shared with advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. The only outbound transfers are the video uploads themselves, sent to the social media platforms on which the operator publishes.

Third-party services

Publishing and measurement are carried out through the official APIs of YouTube, Meta (Facebook and Instagram), and TikTok. Each platform's own privacy policy governs how it handles the content once published.

Children

The application is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with an updated date.

Data Deletion Instructions

Because Comic Capsule has no third-party users and collects no personal information from anyone other than its operator, there is normally no third-party data to delete.
